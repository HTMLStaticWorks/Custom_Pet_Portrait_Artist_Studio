The Commission Experience

From Phone Snapshot to Museum Heirloom

We take the time to know your pet's personality and gaze. Here is how Eleanor Vance transforms everyday camera roll photos into original fine art.

01

Submit Photos & Share Spirit

Send your favorite photos—no professional photography required. Share stories about their personality, energy, and favorite quirks.

02

Composition & Palette Proof

Within 24 hours, receive a digital proof detailing cropped composition, background harmonies, and lighting balance for your review.

03

Hand-Painted by Fine Artists

Crafted stroke-by-stroke with master artist-grade lightfast pigments on Belgian linen canvas or 300gsm 100% cotton paper.

04

Archival Framing & Delivery

Custom-framed in solid American hardwood, sealed against humidity and UV light, and delivered safely to your doorstep with tracked shipping.
