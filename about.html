The Atelier Process

Four small steps. Most patrons finish in one sitting.

It’s a friendly, intimate collaboration — closer to a cozy studio visit than an automated checkout. If you need any advice at any stage, a real artist is just an email or phone call away.

Start Your Commission →
01
Tell us about your pet
Upload a clear photo of your pet and share their name and personality. The studio reviews what you send before we begin work to make sure lighting and angles are optimal.
02
Pick a style & medium
Choose from Classical Oil on Linen, Luminous Watercolor, Digital Fine Art Giclée, or Charcoal — whichever best captures your companion's energy.
03
Review your private proof
Within 24 hours, a private link arrives with your artwork proof. Approve it as-is, or request minor adjustments — unlimited revisions are included until you love it.
04
Receive your framed heirloom
Your finished portrait is prepared for museum archival printing, varnished, custom framed in solid wood if you like, and safely shipped with full insurance and tracking.
Materials & Craftsmanship

Four ways to hang it.

Every commission is handcrafted to last generations using FSC-certified solid hardwoods and acid-free archival mounts.

Classic Wood Frame

FSC-certified solid American Walnut, Natural Oak, or Matte Black with lightweight shatterproof acrylic.

Matted Box Frame

Bevel-cut Snow White museum mount board offering breathing room and depth for watercolor & charcoal.

Linen Float Frame

Stretched Belgian linen canvas suspended inside a deep floating wood tray for an ultra-luxury gallery feel.

Gallery Wrapped Canvas

Clean, frameless 1.5" deep canvas with artwork mirrored around the edges for a contemporary aesthetic.
