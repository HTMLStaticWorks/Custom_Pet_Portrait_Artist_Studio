Frequently Asked Questions

Commission & Payment Terms

How is payment handled?

We require a 50% deposit upon proposal confirmation to secure your spot on Eleanor's painting easel. The remaining 50% balance is only billed after you review and approve the final high-definition digital proof.

Can I purchase a Gift Certificate for a pet parent?

Yes! We offer luxury wax-sealed physical gift vouchers and instant digital vouchers so the recipient can choose their favorite photo, medium, and size directly with the artist.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we safely ship custom pet portraits worldwide via DHL Express with full tracking and transit insurance. Domestic US shipping is complimentary on all Classic and Grand portraits.
